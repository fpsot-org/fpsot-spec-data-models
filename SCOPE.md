# Scope

This Working Group develops the FPSOT data models specification.

The specification covers how FPSOT projects define and publish the data models of their entities: LinkML as the single source of each model, the files generated from it and the checks that keep them in step, the names, keys, types and periods of validity of entities, the binding of codes to controlled vocabularies, names of persons and organizations, postal addresses, telephone numbers and email addresses, audit instants and history, the extensions object of each jurisdiction, versions and deprecation, and persistence mappings. Its first subject area is Party: parties, persons, organizations, party roles, party relationships, contact mechanisms, identifications and classifications. It includes the LinkML Party model, an example jurisdiction profile, examples and a checking script.

It does not cover how a system stores data, how an application programming interface serves the entities, business rules or other behavior, or the content of any controlled vocabulary.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
